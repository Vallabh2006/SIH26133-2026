Essential First Aid Steps

  • Severe Bleeding: Apply firm, direct pressure with a clean cloth. Keep elevated.
  • Unconscious / Fainting: Keep airway clear, place in recovery position on side.
  • Snakebite: Keep victim still and calm. Do not cut or apply tourniquet. Rush to PHC immediately.
  • Burns: Cool with clean running water for 15-20 minutes. Do not apply ice directly.
